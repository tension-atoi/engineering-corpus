# Contributing

Read [governance](docs/en/governance.md) / [gouvernance](docs/fr/governance.md).
Issues and PRs must identify method/lab, exact baseline, claim, primary source,
reproducible fictional example or counterexample, FR/EN impact and verification limits.
Challenges to an oracle are welcome; record disagreements and reasons rather than deleting them.

Run the build, checker, checker negative controls, labs and clean qualification described in README.
Retain machine-readable records in evidence/runs and raw logs in evidence/logs.
Do not submit secrets, private code, user traces, model weights, dependency wheels or large builds.

Methodological changes remain proposals until an explicit scoped owner decision.
Acceptance here ratifies no policy in any consuming project. A reviewer must distinguish
editorial review, executable evidence, browser observation and operational deployment.

## Typography / Typographie

Write punctuation in the document source; the build does not rewrite it. In French prose,
use `« … »` with narrow no-break spaces and French apostrophes (`’`); use narrow no-break
spaces before `;`, `?`, `!` and `:`. In English prose, use curly quotation marks and
apostrophes. Keep code, commands, identifiers, URLs and literal source quotations unchanged.
Do not apply global smart-punctuation conversion: it can alter executable examples and
quoted evidence. Existing pages are not silently normalized by the builder; edits should
follow the language-specific convention as those passages are reviewed.

La ponctuation est écrite dans la source ; le build ne la réécrit pas. En français, utiliser
`« … »` avec des espaces fines insécables et l’apostrophe typographique (`’`), ainsi que
des espaces fines insécables avant `;`, `?`, `!` et `:`. En anglais, utiliser les guillemets
et apostrophes courbes. Le code, les commandes, les identifiants, les URL et les citations
littérales restent inchangés. Ne pas appliquer de conversion typographique globale : elle
peut altérer les exemples exécutables et les preuves citées. Le générateur ne normalise pas
silencieusement les pages existantes ; les passages sont mis en conformité lors de leur revue.
