# CUDA-05G-P01 amendment 01 — unavailable pre-registered container image

The **first attempted acquisition was BLOCKED before any UID/socket observations** because the local Docker daemon no longer had the original experiment image `debian:trixie-slim`. `--pull=never` prevented an implicit download. The acquisition harness had a second, misleading cleanup failure: it attempted `CAP_CHOWN` owner restoration even though the initial owner transfer never succeeded. That was a control-flow error, **not a failed identity experiment**. The setup-failure record is retained in the private evidence directory.

The Docker daemon currently serves a separate Dokploy workload with three containers. Rather than changing that deployment or pulling an image in the background, the already-cached **`postgres:16`** image was inspected and shown to provide the required standard Perl `Socket` and `IO::Socket::UNIX` modules. The experiment executes **only** `perl` and `chown` through explicit entry points; it does not launch PostgreSQL. The preregistered host-UID, socket-DAC, allowed/denied client controls, fail-closed gates and no-network/no-capabilities constraints are unchanged. The **image identity and digest** must be recorded as a changed experimental variable; results cannot be represented as reproduced under the original Debian tag.

Before the next measurement: preflight Docker + cached image + Perl modules before creating the temporary directory; restore owner **only if** it was changed, but preserve failures separately; use a new unique acquisition directory and UTC timestamp. All other experimental restrictions remain as preregistered. This amendment is committed before re-acquisition.
