## Review method and boundaries

For each pair, read the problem, principles/invariants, responsible actor, procedure,
example/counterexample, failure, observable artifact and self-check. Cross-check the
identity/status/reference lists and operational meaning between FR/EN. Automated
metadata parity does not establish translation quality; this pass also read the text.

All original examples remain fictional; no private repository was read for content.
The provided design-system package was used only for typography, palette and interface
rules. Its adoption does not disclose private architecture or ratify corpus methods.
References identify what tool semantics support; they do not make corpus conventions
external standards. No methodological decision is marked `ratified-decision`.

The added lab fixtures are deliberately small, deterministic teaching models. They
do not establish the behavior of real animation systems, Rust APIs, providers,
deployment infrastructure or every Git recovery situation. Exercise time estimates
are editorial estimates, not measured learner completion times.

## Remaining editorial risks

The eight chapters are introductory methods, not comprehensive standards. Reference
links remain susceptible to upstream changes. The lab inventory checks method names,
not arbitrary source/document semantic equivalence. Learner comprehension, usefulness
for a novice and independent FR/EN translation review have not been measured. A PR
review may amend or reject proposals; acceptance ratifies no other project's policy.
